- **A held task shows as held on every surface that names a task's status.** `/audit:status` prints it under its own `[=]` marker with the recorded reason on the line beneath, where it used to fall back to `[?]`. The report and the panel word it `Held` and draw it in a violet of its own (`--st-held`, with an ink, in the theme editor's status group) instead of the unknown style; the panel sorts it after pending and before done, and its touched-tasks line counts it. The ADO state map maps `held` to `Active` by default, as it does `blocked`, and `meta.ado.stateMap` may name it. `/audit:task`'s description, argument hint and the verb reference describe `hold` and `release`, and the journal reference lists `task.hold` and `task.release`.
