- **The red-first proof makes HEAD's baseline run and the task's run at once.** `stamp-verification.py red` runs the two in throwaways of their own - each reset to HEAD with its own home, temp and owner record - and the fix run after both, so the first two legs of a proof cost a little more than one leg's wall time rather than two: two sweeps of `tests/test_stamp_verification.py` side by side took 1.22x and 1.24x one sweep's wall time, measured twice with a gap on a 10-cpu darwin host on 2026-11-27. A stop signal tears down both legs' process groups before either throwaway is removed, the payload's `throwaway.trees` names both, and `MAX_TIMEOUT` leaves room for removing two throwaways rather than one. A leg replayed from the store builds no throwaway, as before. What the two legs cannot keep from each other - state reached by an absolute path or through the git directory the throwaways share - is named in the guide's limits.
