- **A gate's sweep step keeps where its time went.** The recorded per-file counts of a sweep step now carry each file's printed seconds, and the slots it held when more than one, absent when the row printed none; a `sweepBasis` sentence on the step names the three longest files. It is measurement only - no status or verdict reads it - so a gate's 250 seconds can be read off its own record instead of re-run to be found.
